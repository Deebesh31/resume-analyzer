This heuristic is based on the factors below. It is not an employer ATS score or a hiring prediction.

How this score is calculated
  • Resume length: 20 points for 400–800 words; 15 for 300–399 or 801–1,000; otherwise 10.
  • Action verbs: up to 20 points based on detected verb count.
  • Numbers detected: up to 20 points based on detected numeric entries.
  • Contact details: up to 20 points for detected email and phone; phone uses the app’s current text pattern.
  • Average sentence length: up to 20 points based on the current word-count ranges.
